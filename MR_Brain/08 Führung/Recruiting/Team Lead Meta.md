## Stellenbeschreibung

> [!info] Sprache
> Aktuelle Fassung: Englisch (Ausschreibungstext). Deutsche Fassung folgt bei Bedarf.

### Key Responsibilities

- **Disciplinary and Technical Leadership:** Guide, mentor, and continuously develop an agile, highly skilled engineering team within the "Meta" product domain (MetaKIS, MetaIPSS, MetaImplant, MetaDemis).
- **Process Excellence:** Own and optimize standard software development lifecycles using agile methodologies (Scrum/Kanban), ensuring smooth, predictable, and high-quality release cycles.
- **Technical Guidance:** Act as a sparring partner in architectural decisions and drive the development of high-performance software solutions leveraging C#/.NET and Angular.
- **Innovation & AI Integration:** Strategically drive the adoption of cutting-edge AI-assisted development tools within the team's daily workflows to accelerate feature delivery and elevate code quality.
- **Stakeholder Management:** Serve as a key interface between your team, product management, system architects, and senior leadership to align and execute the product roadmap.

### Key Competencies & Experience

- **Leadership Track Record:** Proven experience in the disciplinary and technical management of software engineering teams, with a passion for people development.
- **Core Tech Stack:** Strong, hands-on background in backend architecture using C# paired with solid experience in modern frontend frameworks, specifically Angular.
- **Software Engineering Standards:** Deep familiarity with established SDLC practices, including CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, version control, and agile release management.
- **AI-Driven Workflows:** Practical, hands-on experience utilizing advanced AI development utilities and assistants (e.g., GitHub Copilot, Windsurf, Devin, Claude) to optimize coding workflows.
- **Language Proficiency:** Professional fluency in both German and English (C1 level minimum), enabling seamless communication across local and international stakeholders.

### As a plus

- **Healthcare Domain Expertise:** Initial experience or strong familiarity with the german healthcare system.
- **Clinical Process Knowledge:** Understanding of hospital workflows, particularly in areas such as medical controlling, clinical coding or hygiene management.

## Anforderungen (Kurzfassung)

### Must-have

- Führungserfahrung in Softwareentwicklungs-Teams (disziplinarisch + fachlich)
